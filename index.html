Our guiding vision

Scientific spirituality for a better society

All World Gayatri Pariwar was founded by Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya. Its mission is the awakening of divinity in human beings and the descent of a heavenly atmosphere on Earth.

AWGP Europe carries this vision forward through spiritual practice, cultural upliftment, holistic wellbeing and selfless service.
